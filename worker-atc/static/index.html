Air Traffic Control History

Dedicated to the history of FAA Flight Service Stations.

For more than two decades, atchistory.org gathered the history of the FAA's Flight Service Stations and the people who worked them: facility photographs, training class photos, early airway maps, scanned publications, and first-person life stories. The collection now lives here permanently, preserved exactly as it stood in August 2026. Every old atchistory.org link redirects to its page here.

Start here

  • How the Pilot’s Checklist Came About The 1935 crash of the Boeing Model 299 and the origin of the modern pilot's checklist — the collection's most-read page.
  • Facility Photos Flight Service Stations photographed across the country, organized by state.
  • FSS Class Photos Training class photographs from the FAA Academy, year by year.
  • Historical Aviation Maps U.S. airway maps from the earliest days of the federal airway system.
  • FAA World, 1974–1986 The FAA's employee magazine — the complete monthly run, scanned.
  • Early Radio Years Airway beacons, lighted airways, and the radio range era.
  • Publications & documents 25 more collections: Alaska intercoms, beacon specifications, phone directories, training booklets, and other scanned records.
